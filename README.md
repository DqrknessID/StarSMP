# StarSMP

**StarSMP** is a high-octane, competitive Minecraft survival plugin that introduces a celestial "Star" system. Every player is bound to a random Star, granting them unique cosmic passives and powerful active abilities that define their playstyle and determine their fate in battle.

## 🌌 Core Mechanics

### 1. The Star System
Upon joining or using a Reroll Token, players are assigned one of many unique **Stars** (e.g., *Umbryx, Aetherion, Pyrelion*). Each star has:
- **Passives:** Always-active bonuses (e.g., speed, strength, or unique effects like black holes).
- **Abilities:** Powerful activated skills (Active 1, 2, and 3) that unlock as your Star ascends in Tier.

### 2. Tier Progression
Stars have four Tiers: **Dim (Tier 0)**, **Tier 1**, **Tier 2**, and **Tier 3 (Supernova)**.
- **Ascension:** Get kills or use **Star Essence** to upgrade your Tier and unlock more powerful abilities.
- **Regression:** Dying usually results in your Star's Tier dropping or the Star shattering entirely.

### 3. Cosmic Artifacts
Powerful legendary items forged from the stars themselves:
- **Cosmic Judgement (Mace):** A legendary weapon that can freeze time and launch users into the sky.
- **Spear of the Falling Star:** A swift weapon that grants hyper-speed and bleed effects.
- **Crown of Astral Supremacy:** A godly artifact for those who have mastered the cosmos.

### 4. Custom Economy & Items
- **Star Essence:** Used to upgrade Star Tiers.
- **Reparation Tools:** Used to fix Shattered Stars.
- **Reroll Tokens:** Scramble your celestial alignment for a new Star.

## 🛠️ Features
- **Dynamic Particle Effects:** Immersive visuals for every ability and passive.
- **Combat Integration:** Custom death mechanics, kill rewards, and tier regression.
- **Model Data Support:** Integration with custom resource packs for unique item visuals.
- **Modern Version Support:** Built to handle modern Minecraft 1.21+ features.
- **Specific Compatibility:** The **Spear of the Falling Star** automatically disables itself on versions below 1.21.11 to prevent errors.
- **WIP Artifacts:** Trident, Shield, Crossbow, and Sword artifacts are currently in development.

## 📥 Installation
1. Download the latest `StarSMP.jar`.
2. Place it into your server's `plugins` folder.
3. Restart the server.
4. (Optional) Install the StarSMP Resource Pack for the best visual experience.

## 📜 Commands
- `/star`: Main command for Star selection and management.
- `/stargive <player> <item>`: Give custom items (Admin only).
- `/staradmin`: Administrative controls for tiers and cooldowns.
